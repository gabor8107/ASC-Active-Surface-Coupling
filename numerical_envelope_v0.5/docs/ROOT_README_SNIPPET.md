### New public numerical extension: hybrid energy recovery, v0.5

The [`numerical_envelope_v0.5/`](numerical_envelope_v0.5/) add-on retains the earlier v0.4 public engineering-envelope calculations and adds a reproducible, explicitly **hypothetical** analysis of thermal, RF and unwanted-vibration energy recovery, auxiliary loads, coupling penalties, thermal rejection and mass trade-offs. It includes Python source, tests, CSV/JSON results, 12 graphs and a combined English technical PDF.

**No ASC propulsion, lift, integrated hardware efficiency or experimental validation is claimed.** The 100 kW example is not an ASC power setting; the 4.65 kW *gross* recovered-energy figure is not a net system gain. This release does not disclose the private ASC dossier and does not replace the 28-page Preprint 0.2.
